    Quick start

    1. Pick a brush on the right — Pencil, Ink Pen, Marker, Oil, Watercolor, Airbrush or Chalk — and set its size, opacity and flow.
    2. Paint with a pen, mouse or finger. Pen pressure changes the stroke size and flow automatically.
    3. Use layers to keep sketch, line-art and colors separate. Change a layer's blend mode and opacity in the Layers panel.
    4. Zoom and pan with Ctrl+wheel, a two-finger pinch, or hold Space and drag.
    5. Undo anything with Ctrl+Z. Your work is saved automatically and restored the next time you open the app.
    6. Save a project (Ctrl+S) to keep your layers editable, or Export a PNG, JPEG or WebP to share.
