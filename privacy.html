ChromaCanvas Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

In short: ChromaCanvas Pro does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you draw stays on your own device.

What the app stores

ChromaCanvas Pro keeps your artwork and your preferences (brush settings, colors, theme) locally on your computer, using the browser storage that Windows provides to the app (IndexedDB and local storage). This is what allows your last canvas to reappear when you reopen the app. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Files you open or save

When you open an image or a project, or save or export your work, the app only reads or writes the files you explicitly choose in the Windows file dialog. The app does not scan, index or upload any other files.

Internet access

The app works fully offline. It connects to the internet only to download its own program files when it is first installed or when an update is available. No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services are included, and no cookies are used.

Clipboard

Copying or pasting an image uses the Windows clipboard only when you ask for it (for example with Ctrl+C or Ctrl+V). Clipboard contents are never sent anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
